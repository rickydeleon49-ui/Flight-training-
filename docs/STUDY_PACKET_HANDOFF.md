# Share a study packet

On the Mac with the fast connection:

1. Open the material in Study Library and choose the PDF and pages to use.
   Make the guide, cards and practice test, then review each part. You can
   also make a study kit from the PDF without AI and review its draft questions
   before saving them.
2. Choose **Share or print** → **Study packet (.json)**. Select **Study guide**,
   **Flashcards** and **Practice questions**. Check the counts. Your instructor's
   corrections stay out unless you choose to include them.
3. Choose **Save…** and save the JSON file. Page links and per-item “edited by”
   details don't travel in a packet.
4. Send the file using the channel you choose. The app does not send it for you.

On your Mac:

1. Open **Study Library** → **Import study packet** and choose the JSON file.
2. Compare the guide, card and question counts and look through the preview.
   Choose **Cancel** if you don't want to keep it; nothing is saved.
3. Choose **Add to my Study Library**. It adds a new material. Open the guide,
   cards and practice test and check them against your PDF and current guidance.

The packet works offline. You don't need a Gemini key or a connection to import
or study it. It does **not** carry your PDF, practice history or generation
progress, and importing it does **not** resume a generation.

Send the PDF separately if you want it beside the guide. Importing a packet
will not automatically attach that PDF or reconnect page links. Items pulled
from the sender's PDF are described in the packet's source note; imported
content stays labelled as imported, without an AI or verification claim.

If an import fails, keep the file. Resolve the problem shown and use **Try again**
in the open preview. A retry of the same save does not add another copy. After
closing the app, check the Library for the saved material before importing again.

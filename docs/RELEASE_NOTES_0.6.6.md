## What's new

This update makes the AI helpers faster, simpler and more dependable: one press makes a whole study kit, study guides arrive in about a minute, and "Why?" answers stay short and grounded in your pages.

- **Make my study kit.** One button on a PDF's Study tab makes a basic study guide, about 20 flashcards and about 10 practice questions together, in about a minute. Each part waits for you to read it; save them one by one or with "Save all three". The single buttons are still there for redoing one part.
- **Faster, basic study guides.** A PDF of up to 150 pages is read in one go instead of section by section (a 95-page chapter went from 19 steps to 1). Guides are now short: an overview, then the main topics with a few plain bullets, and technical words explained the first time they appear.
- **Better "Why?" answers.** "Why?" on a flashcard or practice question now reads the text of the pages around the one it cites, so it no longer says the PDF doesn't cover something it does. Answers are 3–6 plain sentences and are never cut off. Under each answer you can see what was sent, e.g. "Sent: this card and the text of pages 14–18" — the PDF itself is not sent.
- **Ask about this PDF.** The app reads the PDF's text on your Mac when you open Ask (nothing is sent), so answers come with their page sources. If something isn't in the PDF, it tells you what the PDF does cover. A bare "why?" gets "Which part do you mean?".
- **Fairer practice questions.** The right answer is spread across A, B, C and D. While you check a new test, answers stay hidden until you press "Show answers".
- **Clearer flashcards.** New cards read "20 new cards — 10 at a time" with "Start 10 new cards".
- **A detailed How to.** Study Library › How to explains every part of a PDF's page, reviewing and practicing, what to do when something goes wrong, and what is sent and when.
- **Fixed: AI stuck "finishing restoring a backup".** If the app's list of AI jobs ever can't be read, it now starts a fresh one and keeps every readable job, instead of refusing AI requests until a restart that never helped.
- **One copy at a time.** Opening Her Flight Deck again brings the open window forward. An older version of the app won't open a library a newer version has used; it asks you to install the newer version and changes nothing.
- **Glass follows your accent.** The Glass appearance's soft background now matches your chosen accent colour.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use an AI helper (Gemini, or Claude if you connect it) or press an airport-weather button. A study kit sends the PDF (or the pages you chose) once.
- No existing data is changed by this update.

## Good to know

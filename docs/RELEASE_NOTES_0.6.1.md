# Her Flight Deck 0.6.1: what's new

Written for her. Paste the "What's new" part into the GitHub release draft above the download table the release workflow writes.

## What's new

This version makes studying from your PDFs calmer, makes it easier to know your homework will work without internet, and adds new ways to practise, from picture cards to oral-exam questions. The four things you'll probably use most:

- **Page notes become cards and questions.** Write a note on a PDF page, then turn it into a flashcard or a practice question.
- **"Before you go offline."** Each homework tells you whether it will work without internet.
- **Better search.** Results are calmer and take you straight to the right place.
- **Practice a mistake again.** After a practice test, review the questions you missed or skipped.

**Study from your PDFs and page notes.**
- **Make a card from a page note.** The card remembers the PDF page it came from. If the PDF has moved or changed, it says "Position unverified" instead of guessing.
- **Make a question from a page note.** It opens in the usual question editor with two blank options and no answer chosen. Fill it in and save; you'll see "Question added to your practice test."
- Study-kit pages repeat themselves less, and the buttons wrap neatly on a narrow window.
- Practice history and your short saved review say which book a question came from.

**Homework: ready for offline.**
- Each open homework shows a "Before you go offline" line, such as "Everything here works without internet" or "2 of 5 need a fix before you study offline." Each activity says whether it works offline or needs a fix.
- The app checks again when you come back to a homework. It never says "ready" while it's still checking.
- Page-range mistakes say which reading and how many pages the PDF has.
- If a PDF isn't ready for search, the notice links straight to "Read this PDF's text".
- Reflection and question activities now have their own answer box, so the prompt isn't filled in as your answer.
- You can remove an activity you haven't saved yet. The editor says "Add activities".

**Search.**
- Results are calmer: three hits first, study materials before topic panels, and no repeated homework titles.
- A homework result shows the homework's name and opens at the right activity.
- Quick Find closes the narrow Menu first and returns you to where you were. Escape closes the Menu.
- Autocorrect is off in both search boxes.

**Practice and mistakes.**
- After a completed practice test, **Short saved review** shows up to five questions you missed or skipped, one at a time, with the answer and explanation as they were when you took the test. Looking at them never changes your score or history.
- After revealing an answer, **Write a note** opens Notes. Nothing is copied in, and a note is created only when you press Save note.
- Adding a card to a set starts fresh reviews for that set. Earlier reviews stay saved with the previous version.

**Flashcards, including picture cards.**
- **"Cover a label" picture cards.** On a PNG picture, cover a label, try to remember it, reveal it, or show the whole picture. Edit them from "Your picture cards". They're left out of exports.
- Cards show "Your card" and "PDF card" counts, and each card has one "Card N options" control.
- Reviewing a card now follows the keyboard, works at large text sizes, and keeps your place.

**Practise an oral question.** On a material page, press "Practice an oral question".
- Save scenarios you want to practise, and mark ones with "My instructor gave me this".
- Practise one question at a time, then show your discussion points.
- Send a scenario to Notes or ask your instructor about it.
- Your answers are not saved.

**Compare flight schools.** In Finances, you can compare schools by name: quotes, aircraft, training track, availability and your own reflections. All of it is optional.

**Log a discovery flight.** The journal has a fresh discovery-flight starting point. Aircraft, instructor and time start blank, and you can add the school's name. Notes-only entries are easy to find in Debriefs, and old drafts are kept.

**Prepare for your next flight.**
- Checklist titles open the lesson, and questions open the exact saved question.
- Printed lesson briefs list your linked homework and whether it's done.
- A lesson page now opens at its top.
- The Daily Plan has a clear Open, Start or Continue button for what to do next, and a calmer line when nothing has a time estimate.

**Airport weather (optional).** Turn on Settings → "Show airport weather". In a flight lesson's Preparation, type an airport's four-letter ICAO code, then press "Get observation" or "Get forecast". You'll see the official NOAA/NWS report, a plain-words summary and how old it is. Only the airport code is sent, and nothing is saved. This is for lesson preparation only. Check official sources and your instructor.

**Exports and printing.**
- Exports and printed pages handle narrow windows and long text better, and printed practice headings stay with their first question.
- Printed lesson briefs include linked homework.

**ACS catalog.** The full FAA-S-ACS-6C list is built in: 61 tasks and every code. You can link study material to a task or code, see how many materials cover each area, and enter a code by hand when you need to. The counts show what reading is tagged. They don't measure how much you've studied or whether you're ready.

**Glass appearance (optional).** Settings has a new Glass look. The usual look is still the default.

**Smaller fixes**
- Small buttons are bigger and easier to hit, and text no longer overflows at large sizes.
- Two flight-hour captions now say "1 flight" correctly.
- Links from a homework stay visible without hovering.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac.
- The app makes a safety copy of your data before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use Gemini or press an airport-weather button.
- This version adds new places to store things, such as saved oral scenarios, picture cards and page-note sources. Nothing you already have is changed or removed.

## Good to know

- Oral-exam answers and airport weather are never saved.
- Airport weather and Gemini need internet; everything else works offline.

**Deviation from the usual process.** The release review process expects a CI-built draft that is then tested. Here, the published asset is the locally built DMG that was already installed and verified. Uploading it unchanged keeps the exact-bytes rule: the published bytes are the tested bytes. A CI rebuild would produce new, untested bytes, so it is not used.

**Visibility.** The repository is private, so the release is visible only to people with repository access. Making the repository public is a separate decision that has not been taken.

## Release notes (as published)

> **Her Flight Deck 0.5.4 for Mac (Apple Silicon)**
>
> **What's new since 0.5.1**
> - **Skills:** track status and confidence for each flight skill, with a history that is kept safe.
> - **Practice:** practice tests you can pause and resume, with your past attempts kept.
> - **Flashcards:** card reviews that bring cards back when they are due.
> - **Follow-ups:** actions and questions from a debrief can link straight to Study Library materials, and remember where they came from.
> - **Study Library:** split a PDF into chapters, and keep track of where each part came from.
> - **Dates:** your local day now shows everywhere, including study material "Added" and "Last reviewed" and the safety-copy lists.
> - **Restoring:** after a restore, the app restarts and comes back to the front on its own. Backups from earlier versions restore cleanly, and your data is upgraded automatically.
>
> **Before you install**
> - Requires an Apple Silicon Mac (M1 or later) and macOS 12 or later.
> - If you already use Her Flight Deck, open it and choose Settings → Save complete backup first.
> - This build is **not signed or notarized by Apple**. macOS will warn that it cannot verify the app. A signed version will follow later.
>
> **Check your download**
>
> `shasum -a 256 -c SHA256SUMS.txt`
>
> SHA-256 for `Her.Flight.Deck_0.5.4_aarch64_LOCAL_ADHOC.dmg`: `1048e4636190d07afcbf2da76fc38822cd13189552b03a0ed69265870857676d`
>
> **Known limits:** not yet tested with VoiceOver, in standard (non-administrator) accounts, or on macOS 12.0–12.2. No Windows version in this release.

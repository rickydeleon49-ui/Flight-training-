## What's new

This update brings the official FAA material into the app, makes the AI study kit more trustworthy, and smooths many small things you ran into.

- **Free FAA handbooks.** Study Library › "Free FAA handbooks" opens the FAA shelf. Get any chapter of the Airplane Flying Handbook from faa.gov, or the whole Pilot's Handbook of Aeronautical Knowledge (about 74 MB) and turn it into study-size parts with one button. Saved copies open offline.
- **Official sources for every checkride topic.** "Practice by checkride topic" now lists the FAA sources for each topic, one per line.
- **Student pilot steps.** Flight Path › Before you enroll has a checklist of the student pilot steps, each with its FAA page. Your ticks are saved.
- **Quote-checked study kits.** Every AI flashcard and practice question is checked against a quote from your PDF before you save it. Practice questions now have three answer choices, like the FAA knowledge test, and show which checkride topic they practise.
- **Video links.** Save YouTube or other video links on each study section and open them from there.
- **Skipped questions.** If you skip questions in a practice test, at the end it offers just those ("You skipped 2. Answer them now?"). Answered questions are never asked again.
- **Keyboard answers keep working.** Keys 1–3 and Enter work in a practice test even after you click with the mouse.
- **Flashcards as a spreadsheet.** A PDF's Share/print dialog now has "Flashcards as a spreadsheet (CSV)…".
- **Clearer progress.** Downloads say "Saving…" when they finish, and making study-size parts shows a seconds counter so you can see it's still working.
- **Smaller fixes.** Saving a study kit no longer ends a practice test you haven't finished; clearer PDF-reading and saving status; one-click "Show answer"; off-topic questions get a hint to ask about the chapter.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use an AI helper (Gemini, or Claude if you connect it), press an airport-weather button, or download an FAA handbook from faa.gov.
- This update adds places to keep video links, checklist ticks, topic tags and FAA downloads. Your existing data is not changed.

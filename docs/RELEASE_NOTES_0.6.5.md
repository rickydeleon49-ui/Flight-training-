## What's new

This update is about trust: your writing and records are safer, the app tells you plainly when something didn't work, and a few screens are calmer.

- **Your writing is safer.** If you quit the app (Cmd-Q, the red button or Quit in the menu), it finishes saving first. Unsaved changes in the study-guide and practice-test editors are kept and offered back next time: "We kept your unsaved changes from last time." Notes you were still typing can never overwrite newer or restored notes — if the saved note changed, the app asks: "Keep these / Use the saved notes".
- **No more doubles.** Pressing Save twice quickly no longer creates two expenses, lessons, goals or actions. While something saves, the form waits and shows "Saving…".
- **Honest safety messages.** If the safety copy before an update couldn't be made, Settings › Your data says so and suggests a complete backup. If a restore didn't go through, you're told once at the next start. If the app ever can't open safely, it explains instead of just closing.
- **Before you enroll.** While you haven't chosen a flight school, Flight Path shows five first steps you can tick off (discovery flight, FAA medical, student pilot certificate, budget, ground study), with links to the FAA pages. Hide it any time; "Show first steps" brings it back.
- **How your practice is going.** Today shows one line from your own practice tests, e.g. "Your last 5 practice tests averaged 78%, up from 70%." It's a trend, not a prediction.
- **Pages without text.** After reading a PDF's text, the app tells you if some pages have little or no searchable text (often blank pages or pictures of text).
- **Calmer screens.** The "Send this file to Google?" dialog is shorter; "Choose fewer pages to send" says what that button does. The material page's actions button is now "File". Status labels read "Review again" and "In Progress". The backup reminder is calmer. Example text in forms starts with "e.g.".
- **Smaller fixes.** Schedule and Finances follow the real day after your Mac sleeps. Reordering milestones and backup details are saved in one step.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use an AI helper (Gemini, or Claude if you connect it) or press an airport-weather button.
- No existing data is changed by this update. Logging out or shutting down the Mac can't wait for the app; your drafts are still kept.

## What's new

This update makes dates impossible to mix up, lets you move around with the keyboard, and makes Settings and the AI dialogs calmer.

- **Dates you type stay the dates you meant.** Every date box now works the same way: type 11052026, 11/05/2026 or 2026-11-05 and the app reads it back in words ("That's Thursday, November 5, 2026."). A date that doesn't exist is refused with a plain message, never changed. Before, typing a date in one go could quietly save a different day. Clicking into a filled date selects it, so typing replaces it.
- **Your profile photo shows.** Choosing a picture in Settings now works.
- **The window opens where you left it**, at the same size, and comes back on screen if a monitor was unplugged.
- **See what changed.** When you regenerate a study guide or practice test and the app asks you to choose, the changed words are highlighted.
- **Sunset and night at a glance.** In the Flight Log, with a date and an airport, a quiet line under Night time shows sunset, when night begins and when night landings count, in your Mac's time.
- **Move around with the keyboard.** The tabs in Study Library, on a material page, in the Flight Log and in Pilot Toolkit work with the arrow keys, Home and End.
- **Claude as an optional AI helper.** Gemini stays the default. If you prefer, Settings → AI provider → "Use a different provider" lets you connect Claude (Anthropic) with your own key; the app always says which helper made a study aid.
- **Calmer screens.** The "Send this file to Google?" dialog is shorter; what Google keeps is one click away in the same dialog. Settings has less repeated text, and both save buttons say "Save profile". Text speaks to you ("your instructor").
- **Smaller fixes.** Forms take you to the first thing that needs fixing. If the app can't check your AI connection, it says so. "Read this PDF's text" sits at the top of More, with a "Read it now" button.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use an AI helper (Gemini, or Claude if you connect it) or press an airport-weather button.
- No existing data is changed by this update. AI keys are kept in separate files on this Mac and are never included in backups.

## Good to know

- This version is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.
- A small private log file in your Mac's Logs folder helps diagnose problems; it never records your notes, file names or keys.

## What's new

This update protects your backups, makes Gemini study guides more dependable, and gives each study material a calmer page.

- **Your backups are protected.** In earlier versions, removing or replacing the file of an FAA reference or a picture-card image could quietly stop complete backups from working. That can no longer happen: the app explains what to do instead. If it had already happened, backups work again, and a restore tells you which pictures or FAA copies need their file added back.
- **Study guides finish reliably.** If Gemini is busy, the app now switches to a backup model and keeps going, tries once more if an answer comes back wrong, and shows progress for each section ("Writing section 2 of 3"). If something still goes wrong, a plain message and **Try again** appear at the top, never a greyed-out button. The draft says which model made it.
- **Gemini 3.8 Flash for new study aids.** New guides, cards and tests use Gemini 3.8 Flash, at the same price. If you chose Standard in Settings, that stays your choice.
- **A calmer material page.** Each material has three tabs: **Study** (guide, flashcards, practice test), **Notes** and **More**. Switching tabs keeps your place in a review or practice test. **Ask about this PDF** opens from one button, and less-used actions, including Delete, are under **More ▾**.
- **A calmer Study Library.** Your materials come first. FAA references and the missed-topics lookup sit together in one shelf below them. A new **How to** tab explains the steps for studying a PDF and how to get around the library.
- **Easier to get around.** New pages open at the top, and going back returns you to where you were in the list. The search box has a clear button.
- **Related study for ACS codes.** Looking up a code from your knowledge-test report now lists your materials tagged with it, and a task code includes its element codes, with the reason shown for each.
- **Smaller fixes.** Reminders no longer pop up by themselves; they live in Pilot Toolkit. Saving a lesson offers **Add preparation**. The Flight Deck no longer says "You are prepared" when no preparation is listed. "Saved." appears after saving a note or picture card. A typed date is read back so you can check it. Buttons are larger and easier to hit.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use Gemini or press an airport-weather button.
- No existing data is changed by this update. If the Pilot Toolkit can't read its saved settings, it now leaves them untouched instead of replacing them.

## Good to know

- This version is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.

# Her Flight Deck 0.6.2: what's new

Written for her. Paste the "What's new" part into the GitHub release draft above the download table.

## What's new

This update fixes a problem where your Gemini flashcards could disappear, and makes Gemini study tools clearer and more reliable.

- **Your flashcards are safe.** Before this update, saving a new study guide for a PDF could replace that PDF's saved Gemini flashcards with an empty set. Saving a guide now never touches your cards, and saving cards never touches your guide.
- **Bring them back.** If this happened to you, the PDF's page now says "Your earlier N flashcards were replaced when a new study guide was saved." Press **Bring them back** to restore exactly those cards. Your current guide stays as it is.
- **Ask about this PDF shows its sources.** Answers about a PDF now include up to three short quotes with page numbers under **Sources**, and the app checks each quote against the PDF's own text first. If the PDF doesn't cover your question, it says so.
- **Flashcards never fail silently.** If Gemini returns no cards, you'll see a plain message and **Try again** — nothing is saved.
- **Cleaner study guides.** Headings and lists in Gemini study guides now display properly.
- **"Why?" and "Explain this" start for you.** They open with a ready question you can edit, and always give an explanation.
- **Related study.** A material's page has a short **Related study** list linking your saved review, questions for your instructor and linked lessons.
- **FAA references.** Keep your Private Pilot ACS and Pilot's Handbook (PHAK) PDFs together in Study Library and open them without internet.
- **Smaller fixes.** A new guide waiting to be saved now appears at the top, greyed buttons say why, and your flashcard count choice is remembered.

## Your data

- Your data stays on this Mac. The app makes a safety copy before it updates.
- Nothing is sent anywhere unless you use Gemini or press an airport-weather button.
- No existing data is changed by this update, except restoring cards when you choose **Bring them back**.

## Good to know

- This version is signed and notarized by Apple, so it opens normally.

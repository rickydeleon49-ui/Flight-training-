Third-party notices for Her Flight Deck
=======================================

This file is copied unchanged into every built app (Vite copies public/ into dist/, and Tauri embeds dist/
in the application). It lists third-party software whose license requires its notice to accompany copies.

Entries are added with the dependency that requires them. Earlier dependencies are not yet listed here;
that pre-existing gap is tracked in docs/STRUCTURED_STUDY_IMPORT_SLICE_A_IMPLEMENTATION_2026-10-03.md.
